Derby

The Memory Vault is based and built here in Derby, and it's one of our most-booked standalone hires in the city — dropped into weddings and family parties from Allestree to Oakwood with no marquee required. Being local means delivery, setup and collection are free, and turnaround on bookings tends to be quicker than further afield.

Nottingham

Nottingham sits inside our free 20-mile Derby radius, so booking the Memory Vault on its own here — for riverside weddings, birthday parties or venue events in West Bridgford and Beeston — comes with no travel charge on top. It works just as well in a marquee as it does tucked into a corner of an existing venue.

Leicester

Leicester's wide mix of weddings and large family and cultural celebrations means the Memory Vault gets used differently here — sometimes a quiet corner for heartfelt messages, sometimes a steady queue at a bigger multi-generational event. Leicester sits just beyond our free 20-mile radius so a transparent travel charge applies, confirmed in your quote, and we also deliver to Oadby, Wigston and Loughborough.

Birmingham

In Birmingham the Memory Vault shows up at both ends of the spectrum — corporate functions and job fairs collecting messages for colleagues, and relaxed family parties in Solihull and Edgbaston. Birmingham is beyond our free 20-mile Derby radius so a transparent travel charge applies, and Sutton Coldfield, Halesowen and Walsall are part of our regular coverage.

Sheffield

Sheffield and the villages on the edge of the Peak District are firmly within our normal coverage, and the Memory Vault travels there just as it does anywhere else — no marquee or décor booking required alongside it.
