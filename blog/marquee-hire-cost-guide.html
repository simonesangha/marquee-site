How much does marquee hire actually cost?

By Sim, White Canvas Events UK  ·  Updated 19 August 2026  ·  6 min read

If you've searched "how much does marquee hire cost" you've probably landed on a lot of vague answers — "£400 to £3,000" with no real explanation of why. We don't think that's very useful, so here are our actual prices for Derby & the Midlands, the same ones our instant calculator gives you, broken down by size with nothing hidden.

The short answer: marquee-only hire starts from £300. Our full packages — marquee, lining, carpet and lighting — start from £514 for a 4m-width marquee and £736 for a 6m-width marquee. Every price covers installation and collection over a full 7-day hire, with delivery free within 20 miles of our Derby base and a transparent travel cost beyond that, confirmed in your quote. Larger marquees for 150+ guests are priced on quotation, because every large event is different. Full breakdown below.

What's actually included in that price

This is where most marquee hire quotes get vague, so to be specific — every price above includes:

  • Delivery to your site — free within 20 miles of our Derby base, with a transparent travel cost beyond that (see below)
  • Full installation and setup
  • Collection afterwards
  • Up to 7 days of hire — not just the one day of your event, so you're not rushing to clear a garden the morning after

A non-refundable deposit secures your booking, with the balance due prior to delivery. Extended hire beyond 7 days is priced on quotation.

What actually changes the price

Once you're past the base package, a few things move the number up or down:

  • Size and guest count. The tables above show this directly — a 4m x 6m and a 6m x 14m are very different builds.
  • Extras beyond the full package. Furniture and finishing touches are priced separately — for reference, Chiavari chairs run from £3.50 each, round tables from £11 each, with premium flooring, drapes, décor styling, heaters and fans priced on quotation depending on scale. Full list on our pricing page.
  • Season and demand. Like most events businesses, peak wedding season (spring through early autumn) books up faster, so earlier enquiries tend to have more date and price flexibility.
  • Your location. Delivery, setup and collection are free within 20 miles of our Derby base. Beyond that, a transparent travel charge applies — covering the extra distance, crew time and the return trip — and it's always confirmed clearly in your quote before you book, never as a surprise on the day.
  • Site access and ground conditions. Every marquee install depends on the site itself — access for vehicles, ground levelness and any power requirements can all factor into the final quote.
