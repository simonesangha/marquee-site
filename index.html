Common questions

Everything you need
to know

What areas do you cover?

We cover the whole of the Midlands — Birmingham, Coventry, Leicester, Nottingham, Derby, Wolverhampton, Stoke-on-Trent and all surrounding areas. Not sure if we reach you? Just ask.

What size marquees do you offer?

We have a 6x12 metre marquee ideal for 50–100+ guests, plus a smaller option perfect for intimate gatherings and home events.

What is dry hire?

Dry hire means you take the marquee structure without setup services. It's our most competitively priced option and gives you complete creative control.

What exactly is the Memory Vault?

A unique cream and gold telephone video box where guests record personal video messages. We package everything and deliver within 5 years — perfect for weddings, new babies or milestone birthdays.

Can I hire the Memory Vault on its own?

Absolutely. The Memory Vault works as standalone hire at any venue — a home, a pub, a corporate space. No marquee needed.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as possible for peak summer weekends. We do our best with shorter notice too — just get in touch and we'll check availability.

Do you cater for smaller home events?

Yes — this is one of our specialities. As celebrations get more intimate and people choose to host at home, we're perfectly set up to bring the experience to you.

What's included in full hire?

Delivery, setup and collection of your marquee. We can also arrange styling, décor, personalised signage and floral touches. Tell us your vision and we'll build the right package.
