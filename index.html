Common questions

Everything you need
to know

What areas do you cover?

We cover the whole of the Midlands including Birmingham, Coventry, Leicester, Nottingham, Derby, Wolverhampton, Stoke-on-Trent and all surrounding areas. Not sure if we reach you? Just ask.

What size marquee do you have?

We have a 6x12 metre marquee — ideal for 50–100+ guests — along with a smaller option perfect for more intimate gatherings and home events.

What is dry hire?

Dry hire means you hire just the marquee structure, without additional styling or setup services. It's our most competitively priced option and gives you full creative control.

What exactly is the Memory Vault?

It's a unique cream and gold telephone video box where guests record personal video messages. We package and preserve everything, then deliver it to you within 5 years — perfect for weddings, new babies, or milestone birthdays.

Can I hire the Memory Vault on its own?

Absolutely. The Memory Vault works as a standalone hire for any event — at home, at a venue, a pub, or anywhere else. It doesn't need to be paired with a marquee.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can for peak summer weekends, but we're flexible. We'll always do our best to accommodate shorter notice bookings — just get in touch and we'll check availability for you.

Do you cater for smaller home events?

Yes — this is one of our specialities. As celebrations become more intimate and people choose to host at home rather than a venue, we're perfectly set up to bring the experience to you.

What's included in a full hire package?

Full hire includes delivery, setup, and collection of your marquee. We can also discuss styling, décor, personalised signage and floral touches. Every event is different — tell us your vision and we'll build the right package.
