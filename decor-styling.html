No marquee required

Décor is a service
on its own — not an add-on

A lot of people assume décor and styling only comes bundled with a marquee. It doesn't — with White Canvas Events, it never has to. We regularly style marquees hired from other companies, existing venues like village halls and barns, gardens with no structure at all, and home celebrations where the room is already there and simply needs dressing.

If you already have a venue sorted and just want it to look right on the day, that's exactly what this page is for. Tell us the space, the date and the occasion, and we'll come back with an honest, itemised quote — no marquee conversation required.
