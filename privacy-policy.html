Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

WhiteCanvas Events UK (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you visit our website, make an enquiry, or book our services.

If you have any questions, you can contact us at:
whitecanvaseventsuk@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Event details (date, location, type of event)
  • Any information submitted through our contact form or email

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we may collect:

  • IP address
  • Device type
  • Browser type
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the site

This is collected through Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and only after you click Accept on our cookie banner.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Provide quotes
  • Manage bookings
  • Improve our website and services
  • Analyse website traffic (only if you accept cookies)
  • Maintain internal records

We do not sell or share your data with third‑party marketers.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

  • Contract — to provide event hire services
  • Legitimate interests — to respond to enquiries
  • Consent — for analytics cookies (GA4)
  • Legal obligation — for accounting and tax purposes

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function
  • Analytics cookies (GA4) — only loaded after consent

You can change or withdraw consent at any time using your browser settings.

For more detail, see our Cookie Policy.

5. How We Store & Protect Your Data

We store your data securely using:

  • Encrypted email
  • Secure servers
  • Access controls
  • Regular security reviews

We keep enquiry information for up to 12 months, and booking information for up to 7 years (legal requirement).

6. Sharing Your Information

We only share your data with:

  • Payment processors (if applicable)
  • Accountants (for legal compliance)
  • Service providers assisting with your event (only when necessary)

We never sell your data.

You have the right to:

  • Access your data

  • Request deletion
  • Object to processing
